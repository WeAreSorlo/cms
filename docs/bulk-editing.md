# Bulk editing

Tick entries in a type's list, across as many of its pages, searches and filters as you like, and Bulk edit opens them in a grid a hundred at a time, a row for each entry and a column for each field. Archive takes them off the live site, and in the archive, Restore brings them back. Singletons have no list, so none of these.

- **Ticks** are kept in the order they were ticked, as the list's pages, sort, search and filter change, until the tab reloads, so they outlast a visit to the grid, an entry or the archive. A list and its archive each keep their own. The box at the top of the table ticks the page.
- **Every entry.** Once the whole page is ticked and the list holds more, a line above the table offers Select all 1,240 entries, or with a search or filter, Select all 37 matching your filter. It ticks every entry they find, after those already ticked, in the list's order, up to 10,000 at a time, and past that it asks to narrow the search or filter. It ticks them as if each had been ticked by hand, so entries added later aren't ticked, entries changed later stay ticked whether or not they still match, and a new search or filter keeps the ticks. Select all becomes Untick all, which unticks every entry of the list, and the line says All 1,240 entries are ticked, or how many are ticked and how many of them are on the page.
- **Columns.** The grid starts with the type's title field and list columns, and Columns picks the rest, remembered in the browser for each type. A group's fields are columns of their own, such as SEO › Title, and so are those of the groups inside it.
- **Cells** show their values as the list does. A click, or Enter or F2, edits a cell in place with its Sorlo Forms input, the only input the grid holds. Repeater, matrix and flexible cells say how many items, rows or blocks they hold, and relationship and media cells what they link to. Each of those opens its whole field in a dialog. A field that a row's conditions hide says Hidden.
- **Keys.** Arrow keys move between cells, Home and End to the ends of a row, and with Ctrl or Cmd to the corners of the grid. Tab moves along a row and leaves the grid at either end. In a cell's input, Enter keeps the change and moves down, and Tab keeps it and moves along once it's past the input's own controls, such as a checkbox field's boxes. Shift+Enter moves up, or starts a new line in long text, and Escape puts back what was there.
- **Checks.** Each row is checked with Sorlo Forms' `validateValues` as it changes, as the entry editor checks its form, with `allowIncomplete` while the type has drafts. Invalid cells show their messages, and a row's status names what's wrong with fields the grid doesn't show.
- **Saving.** Edits stay in the grid until Save, which sends the changed fields of the edited rows that pass their checks, in one request. Publish does the same with every check, and publishes them. A type without drafts has Save alone, which publishes. The rows that pass are saved, each with its version and events. The rest stay highlighted with their messages, and focus goes to the first cell to fix. Since only changed fields go, another editor's changes to an entry's other fields are kept.
- **A hundred at a time.** Bulk edit opens the first hundred ticked, and past a hundred, pages under the grid go through the rest, such as 101–200 of 1,240. They ask first while rows are edited and unsaved, since moving on drops the edits. The grid's address names its hundred, so a reloaded grid shows it without the pages.
- **Archive** ticked entries from the list's panel. It asks first, saying how many of them, or of the pages under them, other entries link to, since those links stop working on the site, and which automations run when entries are archived or unpublished, since each can run once for every entry. It archives a hundred at a time, as Archive in the editor does, with the pages under them, each hundred stamped with one time so restoring one entry still brings back just what was archived with it, and unticks each hundred as it finishes, saying how far it's got. A failure part way leaves the rest ticked, so Archive goes on with them. A run keeps going if you leave the list, and the list shows it as it stands when you come back, so only one runs at a time for each list. Focus moves only on the list, once the button goes with the last of the ticks, and the list moves to its last page when the one it was on has gone.
- **Restore** ticked entries in the archive, a hundred at a time too, asking first only when an automation runs when entries are restored. It restores parents before their pages, and tries again at the end a page whose parent was in a later hundred. A page whose parent stays archived stays ticked, and the panel says so.
- **Not yet:** pasting a range from a spreadsheet, fill-down, undo, setting one field for every ticked entry, Publish or Unpublish for ticked entries without editing them, and ticks that survive a reload.
